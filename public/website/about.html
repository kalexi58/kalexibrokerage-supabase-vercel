About Us

A brokerage that treats security as the service.

Kalexi Brokerage Ltd is a Ugandan brokerage and transaction-facilitation company serving selected commercial sectors.

Who we are

Brokerage, verification and coordination under one roof.

Kalexi Brokerage Ltd is based in Kampala, Uganda. We provide professional brokerage, transaction facilitation, verification, due diligence, coordination and advisory services across petroleum, real estate and investment opportunities.

Our purpose is stated in our mission: Making the transaction secure is our mission. That means helping buyers and sellers find one another, and making sure each can rely on the other before significant commitments are made.

Why Kalexi

Disciplined where transactions are fragile.

Most transaction problems begin with unverified parties, incomplete documents or unclear communication. Our process is designed around preventing them.
