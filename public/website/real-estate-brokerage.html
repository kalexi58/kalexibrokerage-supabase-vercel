Overview

Real Estate Brokerage

Kalexi facilitates property acquisitions and sales across land, residential houses, commercial buildings, industrial property, hotels, rentals and investment property, with verification built into every step.

What it includes

  • Property sourcing and identification
  • Buyer and seller verification
  • Property document review
  • Title verification coordination
  • Property due diligence
  • Site visits
  • Negotiation coordination
  • Property transaction support

How we approach it

Three steps, kept clear.

Step 1

Identify the right property

We source and shortlist opportunities that match what you want to buy, sell or let.

Step 2

Verify parties and documents

Ownership information and supporting documents are reviewed, and title verification is coordinated.

Step 3

Coordinate visits and negotiation

Site visits, discussions and the steps to completion are arranged and tracked.
