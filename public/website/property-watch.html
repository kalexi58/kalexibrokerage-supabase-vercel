The service

Land that is unvisited is land that is exposed.

Many owners hold land they rarely see. Boundary markers move, structures appear, and by the time it is noticed the dispute has already begun.

Property Watch sends a Kalexi inspection officer to your property on an agreed schedule. The officer checks the boundaries and general condition, takes dated photographs and sends you a report. If something looks wrong, you hear about it promptly.

What Property Watch is

  • Scheduled physical inspections with agreed reporting
  • Photographic evidence of the property's condition over time
  • Early notice of unusual activity

What it is not

  • Not 24-hour surveillance or real-time tracking
  • Not guarding or armed security
  • Not a guarantee of protection or a legal service
What is included

Simple, regular, documented.

Scheduled physical inspections

A Kalexi inspection officer visits the property on the agreed schedule.

Boundary observation

Boundary markers, fences and edges are checked and recorded.

Site condition monitoring

Vegetation, access, structures and general condition are noted.

Photographic reports

Dated photographs and written observations after each visit.

Unusual-activity reporting

Client notifications

You are told promptly when something needs your attention.

How an inspection works

From request to report.

  1. Request and scoping

    You tell us where the property is and what you would like monitored.

  2. Availability and agreement

    We confirm whether we can serve the location and agree the inspection schedule and reporting.

  3. Baseline inspection

    A first visit records the boundaries and condition of the property.

  4. Scheduled inspections

    Further visits follow the agreed schedule, with photographs and notes.

  5. Reports and alerts

    You receive each inspection report, and prompt notice of anything unusual.

What you receive

An inspection report after every visit.

Each report is clear and consistent, so you can compare visits and build a record of the property over time.

  • Dated photographs of boundaries and key points
  • Written observations of site condition
  • Notes on any unusual activity
  • A growing monitoring history for the property

Questions

Before you request.

Is this a security or guarding service?

No. Property Watch is scheduled monitoring: periodic inspections with reports. It does not include guards, armed response or continuous surveillance.

Do you monitor my land in real time?

No. Monitoring happens through scheduled visits on a frequency we agree with you, and you receive a report after each one.

Does Property Watch protect me against encroachment?

It helps you notice possible interference earlier, so you can act. It cannot prevent interference or guarantee any outcome, and it is not a legal service.

Which locations do you cover?

Coverage depends on location and capacity. Submit a request and we will confirm whether we can serve your property.

How often are inspections carried out?

The schedule is agreed with each client according to the property and your needs.
