How it works

A clear process from first enquiry to follow-up.

Six stages designed to keep every transaction verified, documented and moving.

01

Initial enquiry

You contact Kalexi with the opportunity, asset or requirement, whether you are buying, selling or seeking an introduction.

What we need from you

A description of what you want to achieve, the sector, and your contact details.

What you get

We confirm we understand the request and explain how we would approach it.

02

Opportunity assessment

We assess scope, parties and viability to decide whether and how Kalexi can help.

What we need from you

Basic details of the asset or product, location and timing.

What you get

A clear view of the next steps and any information still required.

03

Counterparty verification

Before parties are introduced we check identity, business standing and authority to transact.

What we need from you

Identification and business details for the parties involved.

What you get

Any red flags are raised early, before commitments are made.

04

Due diligence and documentation

We review documents and specifications: property records, product specifications and transaction paperwork.

What we need from you

Supporting documents the other party is relying on.

What you get

A clear picture of what has been verified and what remains open.

05

Introductions and coordination

Once verification is satisfactory, we arrange introductions, meetings, negotiations, site visits and refinery or facility meetings.

What we need from you

Availability of the people who need to take part.

What you get

Organised meetings and a documented record of what was agreed.

06

Transaction progress and follow-up

We keep communication moving, track outstanding actions and follow up with the parties until the transaction concludes.

What we need from you

Prompt responses on open items.

What you get

Regular updates so every party knows where the transaction stands.
