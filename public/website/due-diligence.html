Overview

Due Diligence and Verification

Verification is central to how Kalexi works. Before parties are introduced, and again before a transaction progresses, we review who they are, what they hold and what they are offering.

What it includes

  • Buyer verification
  • Seller verification
  • Identity and business checks
  • Property documentation review
  • Counterparty risk checks
  • Fraud red-flag identification
  • Transaction documentation
  • Verification coordination

Kalexi reviews and coordinates verification. It does not issue government certificates or approvals.

How we approach it

Three steps, kept clear.

Step 1

Collect the facts

Identity, business and ownership information is gathered from each party.

Step 2

Review the documents

Documents are examined for consistency, completeness and anything that does not add up.

Step 3

Flag the risks

Red flags and open points are set out clearly so you can decide how to proceed.
